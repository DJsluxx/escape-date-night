What it's for

A quiet evening at home, built around solving something together instead of scrolling next to each other. You print it, set it up in your own kitchen or living room, and play it as a pair — the same puzzle-and-clue shape as a commercial escape room, minus the drive, the booking, and the stranger running the timer.

What's in the download

  • An instant PDF you print at home — no physical product is shipped
  • A sequence of puzzles and clues that lead to a final code or lock, designed for two adults to solve together
  • Plain-language setup instructions, written for someone who has never run one of these before
  • Built for roughly 60–90 minutes of play, including the few minutes of prep beforehand

Exact page-by-page contents and preview images are on the product listing itself — that's the page to check before buying.

What you'll need

  • A printer and scissors
  • A room or two with a few hiding spots
  • Something to "unlock" at the end — a drawer, box, or envelope works fine

Good to know

  • It's a file, not a physical kit — nothing ships, nothing to wait for
  • It's reusable: reprint it for a second attempt later, or pass it to a friend
  • It's not tied to a specific holiday — a normal Tuesday works as well as an anniversary
