New Year's Eve Escape Room at Home: The Countdown Vault

A free, complete printable escape room for two adults. Four sealed cards, one four-digit code, about 10–15 minutes at the table, or 30–45 if you hide the cards around the house. Print it, hide the cards, and open the toast box together before midnight. No signup, no app, nothing to buy.

This is the whole room. All four puzzles and the answer key are on this page. Nothing is held back behind an email address or a purchase.

Before you start (about 10 minutes)

  1. Print this page — it comes out as two or three sheets. The four cards are laid out to print in black and white on a home printer.
  2. Set aside a small box, two glasses, or a drawer as the toast box, and put something small inside — a note, two chocolates, a pair of tickets, whatever suits the evening.
  3. Write the four-digit code (it's in the answer key at the bottom) on a slip of paper and tuck it in with the toast box, so the code is what opens it.
  4. Optional, and it's what makes it a room instead of a worksheet: cut the four cards apart and hide cards two, three and four around one or two rooms, using each answer as the hiding place for the next one — see the hiding map below.

The hiding map (optional)

Every card's answer is a number, so every answer can name the next hiding place. Write down where you put everything before you forget — hiding clues too well is the single most common way an at-home escape room stalls.

  • Card One's answer is a seat number. Hide Card Two at the place setting with that number, or a shelf that many items along.
  • Card Two's answer is a digit. Hide Card Three behind that many books from the left on a shelf.
  • Card Three's answer is a digit. Hide Card Four in a drawer that many down from the top.

The setup

Four glasses were poured for the countdown, and whoever poured them left four cards behind instead of a note. The last card gives you a four-digit number. Solve them in order before the ball drops.

Card One

The Champagne Order

Four glasses are poured at four different times before midnight — 11:00, 11:15, 11:30 and 11:45 — and set at four numbered seats: 1, 3, 5 and 7.

  • Seat 7's glass was poured first, at 11:00.
  • The 11:30 glass is at a lower-numbered seat than the 11:15 glass.
  • Seat 1's glass was poured later than seat 3's.
  • The 11:45 glass is at seat 5.

Which seat's glass was poured at 11:30? That seat number is your first digit.

Card Two

The Favor Boxes

Six sealed favor boxes are numbered. Exactly one of them has digits that add up to 11. Find it.

  • 118
  • 224
  • 335
  • 442
  • 556
  • 617

Take the last digit of that box's number. That's your next digit.

Card Three

The Toast Table

The table is set for the toast. Each of you gets a champagne flute, a party hat and a noisemaker. In the middle there's 1 bottle and 2 sparklers.

Count every object on the table. Take the last digit of that total.

Third digit:    

Card Four

The Countdown Chant

Someone left a short chant by the clock. Read only the first letter of each line:

Toast glasses lifted high,
Embers of the old year fade,
Nothing left but the countdown cry.

The letters spell a word. Count its letters — that's your last digit.

Fourth digit:    

The Toast Box

Put your four digits together in card order. That's the code — open the box together as the countdown hits zero.

Answer key (don't open this until you're stuck, or unless you're the one setting it up)

Card One. Seat 7 takes 11:00 and seat 5 takes 11:45, so seats 1 and 3 hold 11:15 and 11:30 between them. The 11:30 glass must be at the lower-numbered of those two, which is seat 1 — and that also satisfies "seat 1 poured later than seat 3." Seat 1 was poured at 11:30. First digit: 1.

Card Two. 118 → 10, 224 → 8, 335 → 11, 442 → 10, 556 → 16, 617 → 14. The box is 335; its last digit is 5.

Card Three. Three items each for two people is 6, plus 1 bottle and 2 sparklers is 3, giving 9 objects. Last digit: 9.

Card Four. T, E, N spells TEN — three letters. Fourth digit: 3.

The code is 1–5–9–3.

Questions people ask

Is this really free, or is half of it behind an email?
Really free, and complete. Every puzzle and the full answer key are on this page. There is no signup and no account.
How long does it take?
About 10–15 minutes at the table, or 30–45 if you hide the cards around the house, including setup.
What do we need?
A printer, two or three sheets of paper, scissors, and something to use as the toast box. No locks, batteries or props to buy.
Can one of us set it up as a surprise?
Yes — read the answer key, hide the cards, set up the toast box, and play host. It takes about ten minutes.
Does it only work on December 31st?
No. Nothing in the puzzles depends on the date, so it works any evening around the turn of the year.
