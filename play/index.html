A free escape room for two — no printer, no signup, no email. Four puzzles, about 15–20 minutes, playable on your phone or laptop right here on this page.

The Keeper's Watch

You're minding a small coastal lighthouse for a friend tonight, and a storm is coming in. The previous keeper left four notes in the logbook — solve them in order, collect the four numbers, and you'll have the code to relight the lamp before the fog rolls in. Read each note together; one of you can call things out while the other works the answer.

Note One

The Harbor Board

Locked — solve the note above to unlock this one.

One of you read this aloud while the other keeps track on a scrap of paper.

Four boats returned tonight — Merrow, Selkie, Petrel and Kestrel — and moored at four numbered berths: 3, 5, 6 and 8, at four different hours: 5, 6, 7 and 8 o'clock.

  • The boat at berth 8 returned at the earliest hour.
  • The 7 o'clock boat is moored at a lower-numbered berth than the 6 o'clock boat.
  • Berth 3 returned later in the evening than berth 5.
  • The 8 o'clock boat is moored at berth 6.

What hour did berth 3 return? That's your first number.

Note Two

The Tide Chart

Locked — solve Note One to unlock this one.

One of you call out each reading while the other adds up its digits.

Six nights this month, the tide logged its height in centimetres: 143, 158, 172, 186, 191, 205. Exactly one of these numbers has digits that add up to 14. Find it.

Take the second digit of that number. That's your second number.

Note Three

The Supply Shelf

Locked — solve Note Two to unlock this one.

One of you count what's on the shelf while the other counts what's in the corner, then add the two totals together.

The lamp room shelf holds tonight's supplies: 3 tins of oil, 2 spare wicks, 1 brass key, 4 candle stubs and 2 coils of rope. In the corner sit 1 stool and 1 lantern.

Count every object, shelf and corner together. Take the last digit of that total — that's your third number.

Note Four

The Keeper's Note

Locked — solve Note Three to unlock this one.

One of you read each line aloud while the other writes down just the first letter.

Batten the hatches down,
Embers dying in the stove,
Anchor line's holding fast,
Clouds moving fast tonight,
Oil the gears at midnight,
North star's hidden by fog.

Line the letters up in order and they spell a word. Count its letters — that's your fourth number.
