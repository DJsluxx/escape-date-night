Valentine's Escape Room at Home: The Midnight Reservation

A free, complete printable escape room for two adults. Four puzzle cards, one four-digit code, about 10–15 minutes at the table, or 30–45 if you hide the cards around the house. Print it, hide the cards, and open the vault together. No signup, no app, nothing to buy.

This is the whole room. All four puzzles and the answer key are on this page. Nothing is held back behind an email address or a purchase.

No printer handy? Play a different free room online — The Keeper's Watch, right in your browser, no printer needed.

Before you start (about 10 minutes)

  1. Print this page — it comes out as two or three sheets. The four cards are laid out to print in black and white on a home printer.
  2. Pick your vault — an envelope, a biscuit tin, a drawer that's normally off-limits. Put something small inside: a note, a dessert, tickets to something, whatever suits the evening.
  3. Write the four-digit code (it's in the answer key at the bottom) on a slip of paper and tape it inside the vault, so opening it feels like opening it.
  4. Optional, and it's what makes it a room instead of a worksheet: cut the four cards apart and hide cards two, three and four around one or two rooms. Use each answer as the hiding place for the next card — see the hiding map below.

The hiding map (optional)

Each card's answer is a number, so each answer can name the next hiding place. Write down where you put everything before you forget — hiding clues too well is the single most common way an at-home escape room stalls.

  • Card One's answer is a time. Hide Card Two near a clock, or by whatever you reach for at that hour of the evening — the wine rack, the corkscrew drawer.
  • Card Two's answer is a digit. Hide Card Three behind that many books from the left on a shelf.
  • Card Three's answer is a digit. Hide Card Four in a drawer that many down from the top.

The setup

A restaurant that takes one booking a night sent a card to the wrong address — yours. Table seven, tonight, no name on it. Whoever booked it left four cards behind, and the last one is a four-digit code. Solve them in order and the reservation is yours.

Card One

The Reservation Book

Four couples booked four tables tonight — tables 2, 5, 7 and 9 — at four different times: 6:00, 7:00, 8:00 and 9:00.

  • The couple at table 9 took the earliest booking of the night.
  • The 8:00 booking is at a lower-numbered table than the 7:00 booking.
  • Table 2 booked later in the evening than table 5.
  • The 9:00 booking is at table 7.

What time is table 2 booked for? The hour is your first digit.

Card Two

The Wine List

Six bottles are listed by year. Exactly one of them has four digits that add up to 20. Find it.

  • 1968
  • 1974
  • 1982
  • 1996
  • 2003
  • 2011

Take the second digit of that year. That's your next number.

Second digit:    

Card Three

Setting the Table

The table is laid for two. Each of you gets a plate, a napkin, a fork, a knife and a spoon. In the middle there are 2 candles and 1 vase.

Count every object on the table. Take the last digit of that total.

Third digit:    

Card Four

What the Chef Sent Out

The kitchen sent a note with the courses. Read only the first letter of each line:

Rosemary on the lamb,
Oysters to begin,
Saffron through the rice,
Espresso to finish.

The letters spell a word. Count its letters — that's your last digit.

Fourth digit:    

The Vault

Put your four digits together in card order. That's the code to tonight's reservation — go open it.

Answer key (don't open this until you're stuck, or unless you're the one setting it up)

Card One. Table 9 took 6:00 and table 7 took 9:00, so tables 2 and 5 hold 7:00 and 8:00 between them. The 8:00 booking must be at the lower-numbered of those two, which is table 2 — and that also satisfies "table 2 booked later than table 5". Table 2 is at 8:00. First digit: 8.

Card Two. 1968 → 24, 1974 → 21, 1982 → 20, 1996 → 25, 2003 → 5, 2011 → 4. The bottle is 1982; its second digit is 9.

Card Three. Five objects each for two people is 10, plus 2 candles and 1 vase is 3, giving 13 objects. Last digit: 3.

Card Four. R, O, S, E spells ROSE — four letters. Fourth digit: 4.

The code is 8–9–3–4.

Questions people ask

Is this really free, or is half of it behind an email?
Really free, and complete. Every puzzle and the full answer key are on this page. There is no signup and no account.
How long does it take?
About 10–15 minutes at the table, or 30–45 if you hide the cards around the house, including setup.
What do we need?
A printer, two or three sheets of paper, scissors, and something to use as the vault. No locks, batteries or props to buy.
Can one of us set it up as a surprise?
Yes — read the answer key, hide the cards, set up the vault, and play host. It takes about ten minutes.
Does it only work on Valentine's Day?
No. Nothing in the puzzles depends on Valentine's Day, so it plays fine on any evening. Only the framing is seasonal.
