Halloween Escape Room at Home for Adults: The Witching Hour

A free, complete printable escape room for two adults. Four sealed charms, one four-digit code, about 10–15 minutes at the table, or 30–45 if you hide the cards around the house. Print it, hide the charms, and open the cauldron together. No signup, no app, nothing to buy.

This is the whole room. All four puzzles and the answer key are on this page. Nothing is held back behind an email address or a purchase.

No printer handy? Play a different free room online — The Keeper's Watch, right in your browser, no printer needed.

Before you start (about 10 minutes)

  1. Print this page — it comes out as two or three sheets. The four charms are laid out to print in black and white on a home printer.
  2. Set aside a bowl, a small box, or a drawer as the cauldron, and put something small inside — a treat, a note, a small gift, whatever suits the evening.
  3. Write the four-digit code (it's in the answer key at the bottom) on a slip of paper and tuck it into the cauldron, so the code is what opens it.
  4. Optional, and it's what makes it a room instead of a worksheet: cut the four charms apart and hide charms two, three and four around one or two rooms, using each answer as the hiding place for the next one — see the hiding map below.

The hiding map (optional)

Every charm's answer is a number, so every answer can name the next hiding place. Write down where you put everything before you forget — hiding clues too well is the single most common way an at-home escape room stalls.

  • Charm One's answer is an hour. Hide Charm Two near whatever you'd reach for at that hour — the candy bowl, the porch light switch.
  • Charm Two's answer is a digit. Hide Charm Three that many items down in a pile — books, cushions, folded blankets.
  • Charm Three's answer is a digit. Hide Charm Four in a drawer that many down from the top.

The setup

Something's been left bubbling on the porch tonight — a covered bowl that won't open until the coven's number is found. Four sealed charms are hidden nearby, and the last one gives you the code. Break the seals in order.

Charm One

The Trick-or-Treat Ledger

Four trick-or-treaters visited four houses tonight — houses numbered 3, 6, 8 and 10 — at four different times: 5:00, 6:00, 7:00 and 8:00.

  • House 10 got the earliest visit of the night.
  • The 7:00 visit is at a lower-numbered house than the 6:00 visit.
  • House 3 was visited later in the evening than house 6.
  • The 8:00 visit is at house 8.

What time is house 3 visited? The hour is your first digit.

Charm Two

The Pumpkin Patch

Six carved pumpkins are numbered by weight in pounds. Exactly one of them has digits that add up to 12. Find it.

  • 108
  • 127
  • 155
  • 183
  • 206
  • 214

Take the last digit of that weight. That's your next number.

Charm Three

The Porch Count

Two trick-or-treaters come up the steps. Each one carries a candy bag and wears a glow-stick necklace. On the porch itself there are 4 carved pumpkins and 3 paper bats hanging from the door.

Count every object — bags, necklaces, pumpkins, bats. Take the last digit of that total.

Third digit:    

Charm Four

The Whisper at the Door

Someone left a note taped to the frame. Read only the first letter of each line:

Moonlight spills across the yard,
Owls perched, keeping guard,
Old gate creaks, opened wide,
Nothing waits for you inside.

The letters spell a word. Count its letters — that's your last digit.

Fourth digit:    

The Cauldron

Put your four digits together in charm order. That's the number that opens the cauldron — go open it.

Answer key (don't open this until you're stuck, or unless you're the one setting it up)

Charm One. House 10 takes 5:00 and house 8 takes 8:00, so houses 3 and 6 hold 6:00 and 7:00 between them. The 7:00 visit must be at the lower-numbered of those two, which is house 3 — and that also satisfies "house 3 later than house 6." House 3 is at 7:00. First digit: 7.

Charm Two. 108 → 9, 127 → 10, 155 → 11, 183 → 12, 206 → 8, 214 → 7. The pumpkin is 183; its last digit is 3.

Charm Three. Two items each for two people is 4, plus 4 pumpkins and 3 bats is 11. Last digit: 1.

Charm Four. M, O, O, N spells MOON — four letters. Fourth digit: 4.

The code is 7–3–1–4.

Questions people ask

Is this really free, or is half of it behind an email?
Really free, and complete. Every puzzle and the full answer key are on this page. There is no signup and no account.
Is it for adults or for kids?
It's written for two adults on a quiet evening, and it's strictly PG — spooky, not scary. If you want one for a children's party, our sister site has printable escape room kits for ages 4–9.
How long does it take?
About 10–15 minutes at the table, or 30–45 if you hide the cards around the house, including setup.
What do we need?
A printer, two or three sheets of paper, scissors, and something to use as the cauldron. No locks, batteries or props to buy.
Do we have to play it on Halloween itself?
No. Nothing in the puzzles depends on October 31st, so it works any night of the season.
